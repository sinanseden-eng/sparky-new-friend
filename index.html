Sparky's New Friend

Sparky is a robot. Dr. Vic made him in a lab. Sparky is big and silver. He has blue bolts. He has a round light on his chest. It is his heart-light. Today, the light is blue. Sparky is lonely. He wants a friend.

"Go find a friend," says Dr. Vic. Sparky goes outside. He sees three paths.
